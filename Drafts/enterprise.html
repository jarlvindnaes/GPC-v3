The platform

One data model, from raw material to point of sale.

Components are the authored unit — products are how humans group them. Passports are minted per unit, from real sales orders. That architecture is the difference between a filing system and a living data layer.

Components
The atomic unit. Legs, seats, fabrics, fittings, packaging — each with material data, origin, supplier, and CO2 contribution. LCA runs at this level, so every footprint is decomposable and defensible.
Products
Structured families, not flat SKU lists. One product family, axes of variation visible (frame finish × upholstery × armrest). 3D digital twins per family member — ~13 models for a full catalogue, not 300.
Passports
Minted per unit, from your ERP. When a sales order ships, a Digital Product Passport is created for that physical unit — ESPR-compliant, QR-addressable, and tied to the exact component data it was built from.
Outputs
EPDs, ESG reporting, customer-facing pages. Verified EPDs (third-party or GPC-verified), heat maps and Sankey diagrams for hotspot analysis, and product pages that turn compliance data into customer experience.
