How it works

From your website to a working catalogue in 60 seconds.

No imports, no spreadsheets, no months of setup. You start with a real, structured catalogue and increase the resolution from there.

https://yourcompany.com → 13 products, 4 suppliers, estimated BOMs — scanned, structured, and ready to refine.
1

Paste your URL

We scan your website and build your catalogue automatically — products, variants, materials, and the suppliers we find in your own content. Nothing is mocked: every field is tagged with its source.

2

Deepen the data

Replace estimates with real data, in place. Invite suppliers to verify their components. Upload 3D files. The structure you saw on day 1 is the same structure that carries real passports later.

3

Publish passports

Generate EPDs and ESPR-compliant Digital Product Passports. Each product gets a customer-facing page with a QR code — CO2 footprint, materials, and spare parts included.
