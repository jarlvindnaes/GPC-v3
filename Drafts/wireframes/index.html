Component-level by design

Measure a component once. Reuse it everywhere.

We gather data and calculate LCA at the component level - a leg, a screw, a fabric - not just the finished product. So the work compounds: every product that shares a component inherits its data automatically, with nothing re-typed.

The passport

Not just a web page. A passport that proves itself.

Every product gets a beautiful, mobile-first page behind a QR code or NFC tag: footprint, materials, origin, care instructions - and a direct line to spare parts. A scan - or a tap - becomes a customer relationship.

  • Provably genuine - digitally signed, so anyone can confirm it's real in one tap. Not just a web page someone could fake.
  • Consumer-facing, mobile-optimised, on-brand
  • QR code or NFC tag - your choice; customers just scan or tap
  • Spare parts & upgrades sold straight from the passport

Issued as cryptographically signed credentials (did:web) and checked by an independent third party (EECC) - the authenticity standard set out in EU ESPR 2024/1781.
