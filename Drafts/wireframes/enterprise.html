Supply chain mapping

From raw material to living room - on one map.

Capabilities

Everything the floor needs - nothing siloed.

Each capability plugs into the same product graph. No separate tools, no data silos, no re-keying.

⊞

Component-level LCA & reuse

Impact calculated per component and reused across every product that shares it - measure once, never re-key. Upload a 3D model and shared components are detected automatically. EN 15804-aligned.

⟐

ERP integration & DPP minting

Mint Digital Product Passports straight from your ERP/PLM. Data stays in sync, not in spreadsheets.

◍

Supplier verification at scale

Invite multi-tier suppliers to verify their own components - first-hand, primary data straight from the source. Provenance tracked on every value, so data quality is provable, not asserted.

⚗

Impact simulator

Swap suppliers, materials, or origins and see the environmental impact before you commit.

▤

Supply-chain analytics

Concentration risk, geopolitical exposure, and alerts when a route or material gets vulnerable.

⇄

Customs & cross-border

Auto-generate HS codes, origin certificates, and duty calculations from your product graph.

Verified, not estimated

End-to-end verified EPDs - audit-ready by default.

Suppliers verify their own components directly in the platform - first-hand, primary data that no consultant can match. Every value carries its provenance: extracted, estimated, authored, or supplier-verified. Our verification concierge then takes EPDs all the way to third-party sign-off - in batches, at portfolio scale.
