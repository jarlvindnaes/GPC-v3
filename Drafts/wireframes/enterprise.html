Verified, not estimated

End-to-end verified EPDs - audit-ready by default.

Suppliers verify their own components directly in the platform - first-hand, primary data that no consultant can match. Every value carries its provenance: extracted, estimated, authored, or supplier-verified. Our verification concierge then takes EPDs all the way to third-party sign-off - in batches, at portfolio scale. The calculation methodology and the platform itself are independently audited, and every result traces back to its inputs - so your LCAs are auditable end to end, not a black box.
