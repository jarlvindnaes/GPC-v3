Capabilities

Trust & compliance

Audit-ready, current, portable, and secure.

The reassurance procurement asks for - answered, without the spreadsheet risk.

Verified, not estimated

Suppliers verify their own components - first-hand data no consultant can match. Our concierge takes EPDs to third-party sign-off, and the methodology itself is independently audited.

Keeps pace with regulation

We maintain the Product Category Rules and methods. ESPR, CBAM and EN-standard changes roll across your catalogue, so your data stays compliant without rework.

Your data stays yours

Export everything at any time in open standards - EN 15804, GS1, did:web. An asset you own, not a dependency you rent. No lock-in.

Secure by design

EU-hosted and GDPR-aligned, encrypted in transit and at rest, automated backups, and tamper-evident did:web credentials.
