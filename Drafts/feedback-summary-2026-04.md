# GPC Front Page — Feedback Summary (April 2026)

**Site reviewed:** https://xbuild.com/productconnect-website/
**Reviewers:** Colleague + Ida (multimedia student, webshop focus, no prior GPC knowledge)

---

## What's working

- Site feels professional.
- **"How it works"** is a strong section — a good way to describe a complex product.
- The fact that the site will usually appear alongside a pitch/conversation buys you some slack on complexity.

---

## Proposed changes, ordered by impact

### 1. Make "what it is" and "who it's for" instantly obvious
Both reviewers landed on the same core issue: a cold visitor has to click around to assemble an understanding of what Product Connect actually is. Fix this in the hero or directly beneath it with a plain-language one-liner:

> *Product Connect is [category] for [audience], helping them [outcome].*

### 2. Restructure the page around a clearer narrative
Current flow feels feature-led. Move to a value-led flow:

1. What is it
2. Who is it for
3. How does it work (keep this — it's good)
4. What value does it create
5. What's the next step

### 3. Lead with value, not features
Lift the "Value for everyone in the chain" content from the DPP subpage up onto the front page. It does the concrete "what do I actually get" work better than the current Gains section.

### 4. Trim the Gains
Drop Gain 4, or restructure to **3 gains + 1 future vision**. Right now it reads as overselling.

### 5. Sharpen the audience
The page doesn't clearly differentiate who it's speaking to. Consider segmenting (e.g. manufacturer vs. retailer vs. compliance/legal) so each reader can self-identify.

### 6. Add an onboarding section
Reviewers want to know *what happens and when*. A simple timeline works well:

- What we do in week 1
- What you see in month 1
- When the "effect" actually lands

This speaks directly to the buyer and removes ambiguity about commitment.

### 7. Housekeeping
- **Add a logo** — still missing.
- **Hold off on testimonials** at this stage — too early, and they draw the wrong kind of scrutiny.

---

## What to keep untouched

- "How it works" section — reviewers explicitly praised it.
- Overall visual polish — the professional feel is landing.

---

## Suggested next step

Pick the top 2–3 items from this list to tackle in the next iteration. My recommendation: **#1 (hero clarity)**, **#3 (value up front)**, and **#4 (trim Gains)** — they're the ones that directly address the "I don't get it fast enough" problem both reviewers flagged.
