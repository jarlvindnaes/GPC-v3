# Product Connect — Next generation

Two marketing entry points: `/` for furniture brands and `/enterprise/` for larger furniture and building-product manufacturers. Built on the requested `astro-migration` branch, in `copy-edits`.

The furniture story centres on carbon footprint, a Digital Product Passport and aftercare. Enterprise centres on a shared component-level model, reuse across products, data provenance and cross-team use. Copy is English, following the supplied sketches. The contact CTA opens a prepared email; it does not send anything. The existing `/dpp/` experience remains available as a sample.

Design: charcoal and lime, oversized restrained type, editorial serif accents, original furniture imagery and an explicit data relationship diagram. No invented customer testimonials, metrics, prices or regulatory deadlines were added.

Sources: supplied xbuild wireframes; `Drafts/feedback-summary-2026-04.md`; Obsidian GPC landing-page redesign and index notes. Jan’s concept is represented through the existing wireframe’s physical-product-to-digital-identity story; his original PDF was not independently reviewed.

Generated asset: `public/nextgen/chair.png`, built-in imagegen, original illustrative product rather than a named customer product. Prompt: photorealistic luxury editorial oak dining chair with curved back and cream wool upholstery, full chair right-centred in dark charcoal architectural studio, directional light, no text or logos.

Before a public launch, confirm the live contact address (hello@getproductconnect.com), approved claims and whether to retain the old sample DPP and legacy routes. They are preserved here. Dependency installation reported 19 existing audit findings; no broad dependency upgrades were made as part of this visual redesign.

Validation: production build, Astro and TypeScript checks, static route/anchor/asset checks. No browser interaction or visual QA was requested or performed. Local preview opened through Codex.
