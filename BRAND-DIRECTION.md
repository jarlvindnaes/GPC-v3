# Product Connect — Signal

A visual identity built around a physical object and the knowledge inside it.

## Colour

| Role | Colour | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | #111B30 | Brand surfaces and confident headlines |
| Paper | #F7F8FB | Clear space around technical information |
| Data | #697CF5 | Connections, selected controls and component information |
| Signal | #D4EE9C | Primary calls to action and a restrained brand accent |
| Muted | #8190A6 | Secondary labels |
| Rule | #DFE4ED | Fine grids, dividers and product panels |

## Typography and composition

Inter carries the identity, using substantial but restrained headlines, clean body text and compact technical labels. Monospaced metadata distinguishes the model and its state. Pale precise product surfaces alternate with substantial ink sections. Grids describe relationships, rather than filling empty space.

## The signature movement

The product begins assembled, separates into its real modelled parts, pauses to reveal their story, and assembles again. The information connects the parts to the product through three chapters: product, components, connection.

Scroll controls the sequence. Playback, chapter buttons and a scrubber provide direct control. Reduced-motion preferences disable automatic scroll movement. The supplied Cross Chair has 13 separately modelled parts; the accompanying data relationships are illustrative and make no measured environmental claims.

## Reference interpretation

Stripe informs hierarchy and the presentation of a substantial platform. Attio informs typographic restraint and precise information surfaces. Keystone informs the connection between component knowledge and product outcomes. Product Connect keeps its own colours, language and product assets.
